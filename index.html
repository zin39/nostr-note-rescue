Nostr Note Rescue

"Where did my posts go?" Paste your npub. This page asks many relays for your notes, shows which relays have which ones, and can rebroadcast the missing signed notes to the relays you choose. No private key needed, because notes are already signed by you and relays accept exact copies. Everything runs in your browser.


Rebroadcast: send every note found anywhere to the relays checked below that are missing it.
